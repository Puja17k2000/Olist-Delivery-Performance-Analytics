# Stage 5 Findings: Delivery Delay Analysis

## KPIs (96,470 delivered orders)
- On-time delivery: 93.23%
- Avg delivery time: 12.6 days; avg delay of late orders: 10.6 days
- GMV: R$ 15.4M; AOV: R$ 159.83

## Findings
1. Any delay hurts reviews: on-time 4.29, 1-3 days late 3.29, 4-7 days late 2.10, 8+ days late about 1.7.
2. North-east states have the highest late %: MA 17.4%, CE 13.8%, BA 12.2%. SP is lowest among large states at 4.5%.
3. RJ (12.1% late, 12,350 orders) and SP (4.5% late, 40,494 orders) each have about 1,500-1,800 late orders, so volume matters as much as rate.
4. Late % spiked in 2017-11 (12.4%), 2018-02 (14.1%) and 2018-03 (19.0%). The Feb-Mar 2018 spike is not explained by order volume.
5. Long delivery time does not mean late: PA has the longest delivery (23.8 days) but 11.2% late.
