# Stage 6 Findings: Seller Scorecard

Scope: 627 sellers with at least 30 delivered orders (81,647 seller-orders, 5,485 late). An order with several sellers is counted once per seller.

1. Seller performance varies a lot: worst 10% of sellers are late on 16.22% of orders, best 10% on 0.55%.
2. The problem is spread out, not concentrated: worst 10% of sellers account for 15.6% of late orders, worst 20% for 30.0%, worst 30% for 53.9%.
3. Scenario: bringing the worst 10% of sellers to the average late rate (6.72%) would remove about 500 late orders (about 9%). For the worst 20% it is about 15%.
4. Caution: top-10 worst sellers have only 30-96 orders each, so individual rates are noisy. Report deciles, not names.
