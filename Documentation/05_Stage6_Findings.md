# Stage 6 Findings: Seller Scorecard

Scope: 627 sellers with at least 30 delivered orders (81,647 seller-orders, 5,485 late). An order with several sellers is counted once per seller.

1. Seller performance varies a lot: worst 10% of sellers are late on 16.22% of orders, best 10% on 0.55%.
2. The problem is spread out, not concentrated: worst 10% of sellers account for 15.6% of late orders, worst 20% for 30.0%, worst 30% for 53.9%.
3. Scenario: bringing the worst 10% of sellers to the average late rate (6.72%) would remove about 500 late orders (about 9%). For the worst 20% it is about 15%.
4. Caution: top-10 worst sellers have only 30-96 orders each, so individual rates are noisy. Report deciles, not names.


## Seller patterns
| Group | Sellers | Orders | GMV share | Late % | Avg handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Worst 10% | 63 | 5,259 | n/a | 16.22% | 4.6 days |
| Worst 20% | 126 | 12,397 | 16.3% | 13.26% | n/a |
| Other 80% | 501 | 69,250 | 83.7% | 5.55% | 2.1-3.2 days |
| Best 10% | 62 | 4,189 | n/a | 0.55% | 2.1 days |

- The worst 10% of sellers take about 2x longer to hand over parcels (4.6 vs 2.1 days) and have lower reviews (3.79 vs 4.35).
- Seller state is not a clean quality signal: SP sellers ship nationwide, so their late % (7.2%) mixes seller quality and route length.
- Worst 20% of sellers give 16.3% of scorecard GMV, so delisting is costly. Recommendation: seller improvement program (handling-time target) instead of removal.
- Scenario: bringing the worst 20% to the other sellers' late rate (5.55%) would remove about 950 late orders (about 15% of all 6,534). Best-case estimate, not a guarantee.
