# Business Brief: Olist Delivery Performance Analytics

## Company
Olist is a Brazilian marketplace that connects small sellers to customers across the country.

## Business problem
Leadership believes late deliveries are lowering customer review scores and hurting repeat purchases.

## Objectives
1. Measure current delivery performance (on-time %, average delay).
2. Identify where and why delays happen (state, seller, category, distance).
3. Quantify how delays affect review scores.
4. Identify the worst-performing sellers.
5. Forecast monthly revenue for planning.
6. Recommend actions with estimated impact.

## Stakeholders
- Head of Operations
- Seller Success team
- Executive team

## KPIs
| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| GMV | Total value of orders (item price + freight) |
| AOV | GMV / number of orders |
| On-time delivery % | Orders delivered on or before the estimated date |
| Average delay days | Average days late, for late orders only |
| Average review score | Mean of review scores (1 to 5) |
| Repeat purchase rate | Customers with 2+ orders / total unique customers |

## Data
9 tables from the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (about 100K orders, 2016 to 2018).
